Our Mission & Vision

Nurturing Bengali Heritage with Contemporary Excellence

Our commitment to child-centric holistic education rooted in Bengali soil.

Our Mission (আমাদের লক্ষ্য)

To provide accessible, high-caliber Bengali-medium schooling from early childhood (Class 0 / Nursery) to Primary (Class V), fostering analytical thinking, dual fluency in Bengali & English, scientific inquiry, and humanitarian empathy.

Our Vision (আমাদের দর্শন)

To be West Bengal's leading model primary institution where mother tongue education is celebrated as the strongest cognitive foundation, empowering young learners to step confidently into secondary high schools and the digital world.

Core Values (মূল্যবোধ ও নীতি)

Discipline with compassion, respect for elders and teachers, scientific curiosity, artistic self-expression, honesty, environmental care, and cultural pride inspired by Rabindranath Tagore and Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar.

About Us

Dedicated to Foundational Excellence in Bengal

Our Inception & Guiding Principles

Founded in 2012 in the culturally rich soil of West Bengal, M R Public Mission was established with a singular vision: to prove that children learn, imagine, and excel fastest when their foundational years are anchored in their mother tongue—Bengali—supplemented with modern English and digital competencies.

From pre-primary Class 0 (Nursery) to Class VI, we provide a joyful, safe sanctuary where traditional Bengali respect, arts, and values blend harmoniously with interactive pedagogy, smart classes, and science laboratories.

Holistic Bengal Board Curriculum: Aligned with West Bengal primary educational benchmarks.
Child-Centric Pedagogy: No rote learning; focus on conceptual understanding, storytelling, and activities.
Parent-School Partnership: Continuous feedback via diary, quarterly PTMs, and active counseling.

School Rules & Code of Conduct

1
Punctuality: Students must arrive by 8:15 AM. Gates close promptly at 8:25 AM for morning assembly and prayers.
2
Uniform & Grooming: Students must wear clean, ironed school uniform with ID cards daily. Nails must be trimmed and hair neatly combed.
3
School Diary: The school diary must be checked and counter-signed by parents every evening for homework notes or teacher messages.
4
Attendance & Leave: Minimum 80% attendance is mandatory. Leave applications must be submitted in advance through the proforma form.
5
Parent-Teacher Meetings: Attendance in quarterly PTMs is compulsory to ensure collaborative child growth.
